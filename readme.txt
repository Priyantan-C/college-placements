Placement charts and year-wise student placement tables for St. Thomas' College of Engineering & Technology.

== Description ==

Admin staff upload the year's student placement Excel file. The plugin counts offers per company, merges different spellings of the same company, and draws the placement charts and the student tables on the site.

Shortcodes:

* `[stcet_placement_trend]` – placements over the years.
* `[stcet_placement_year]` – year picker, pie chart, totals and company table.
* `[stcet_placement_records]` – year-wise student tables (`years="2026,2025"`, `open="first|all|none"`).
